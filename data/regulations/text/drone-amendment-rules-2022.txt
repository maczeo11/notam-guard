# The Drone (Amendment) Rules, 2022
### Notification
MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION. NOTIFICATION. New Delhi, the 11th February, 2022. Drone (Amendment) Rules, 2022.
G.S.R. 108(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5, sub-section 2 of section 10 and sections 10A, 10B and 12A of the Aircraft Act, 1934 (22 of 1934), and after dispensing with the condition of previous publication in the public interest as required under the proviso to section 14 thereof, the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend the Drone Rules, 2021, namely:—
### 1. Short title and commencement
(1) These rules may be called the Drone (Amendment) Rules, 2022.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
### 2. Amendment of rule 3
In rule 3 of the Drone Rules, 2021 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in sub-rule (1), for clause (t), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:-
‘(t) “Remote Pilot Certificate” means the certificate issued by an authorised remote pilot training organisation to any individual under rule 34;’.
### 3. Amendment of rule 16
In rule 16 of the said rules, in sub-rule (1), for the words “within a period of thirty-one days falling after the said date”, the words, figures and letters “on or before the thirty-first day of March, 2022,” shall be substituted.

### 4. Amendment of Part VI
In Part VI of the said rules, for the heading occurring under that Part, the heading “REMOTE PILOT CERTIFICATE” shall be substituted and for the word “licence” wherever it occurs, the word “certificate” shall be substituted.
### 5. Amendment of Parts VII, VIII and XII
In Parts VII, VIII and Part XII of the said rules, for the word “licence” wherever it occurs, the word “certificate” shall be substituted.
### 6. Amendment of rule 34
In rule 34 of the said rules, sub-rule (4) shall be omitted.
### 7. Amendment of rule 35
In rule 35 of the said rules, in clause (c), for the words “Director General”, the words “authorized remote pilot training organization” shall be substituted.
### 8. Amendment of rule 53
In rule 53 of the said rules, the word “licence” shall be omitted.
### 9. Amendment of Forms D-1 and D-2
In Forms D-1 and D-2 of the said rules, in Part B, after serial number 15 and before Part C, the following Note shall be inserted, namely:-
“Note.- Serial numbers 10, 11 and DIN in serial number 12 shall not apply to any Ministry, Department, police force or institution under the control of the Central Government or State Government.”.
### 10. Amendment of Form D-3
In Form D-3 of the said rules, in Part C, after serial number 28 and before Part D, the following Note shall be inserted, namely:-
“Note.- Serial numbers 23, 24 and DIN in serial number 25 shall not apply to any Ministry, Department, police force or institution under the control of the Central Government or State Government.”.
### 11. Amendment of Form D-4
In Form D-4 of the said rules, in the heading, for the word “LICENCE”, the word “CERTIFICATE” shall be substituted.
### 12. Amendment of Form D-5
In Form D-5 of the said rules, in Part B, after serial number 15 and before Part C, the following Note shall be inserted, namely:-
“Note.- Serial numbers 10, 11 and DIN in serial number 12 shall not apply to any Ministry, Department, police force or institution under the control of the Central Government or State Government.”.
### Foot note
The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-section (i) vide notification number G.S.R. 589(E), dated the 25th August, 2021.
