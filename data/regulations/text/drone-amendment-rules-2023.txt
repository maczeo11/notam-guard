# The Drone (Amendment) Rules, 2023
### Notification
MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION. NOTIFICATION. New Delhi, the 27th September, 2023.
G.S.R. 715(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5, sub-section 2 of section 10 and sections 10A, 10B and 12A of the Aircraft Act, 1934 (22 of 1934), and after dispensing with the condition of previous publication in the public interest as required under the proviso to section 14 thereof, the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend the Drone Rules, 2021, namely:—
### 1. Short title and commencement
(1) These rules may be called the Drone (Amendment) Rules, 2023.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
### 2. Amendment of Form D-4
In Form D-4 annexed to the Drone Rules, 2021, under Part A relating to details of individual, for the entry against Serial number 5, the following entries shall be substituted, namely :-
“5. Indian Passport Number or government issued proof of identity and government issued proof of address i.e., a Voter’s ID card, Ration Card or Driving License”.
### Note
The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-section (i) vide notification number G.S.R. 589(E), dated the 25th August, 2021.
